pagode brasileiro, roda de samba, 110 bpm, loud punchy percussion upfront in the mix, surdo, tantã, repique de mão, pandeiro, cavaquinho, banjo, loud hand claps, party energy, major key, call and response, cheerful girl and boy child vocals, kids choir shouts, Brazilian Portuguese

EXCLUIR (campo "Exclude"):
slow, sad, ballad, explicit, soft drums

(Laiá, laiá!)
